### Where the credits are in the app

The app reads `/v2/targets` and draws trains, so Settings > About > Data and credits carries every credit above (the words
are `DataCredits` in `Packages/BoatCore`, asserted verbatim by its tests): boats via aisstream.io, trains estimated from
MTA's real-time arrival predictions with "Transit data: MTA. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the MTA. Data provided as
is and may be inaccurate, incomplete or delayed." and a link to mta.info/developers, Apple Maps, NYC Open Data, Census
TIGER/Line, USGS and "© OpenStreetMap contributors" (link to its copyright page), and "Positions can be late or wrong.
Don't use this app for navigation or safety." When the newest train position is over a minute old (boats: five), the
status line says "Trains last updated 2 min ago" instead of "Live", as MTA's terms ask. Change a credit in `DataCredits`
and here together.

- Do not draw MTA's route bullets or use the MTA name or symbol as branding unless you hold an MTA license: MTA lists
  "NYCT subway route indicators" as its intellectual property.

## Derived data in this repository

The derived data above (`Packages/BoatCore/.../HarborSurface.swift`, `relay/src/subway-data.ts`,
`relay/data/elevation.json`) comes with its source's terms, not the project's. The test fixtures are not part of that:
they are synthetic (see "Test data" in `relay/README.md`), so no provider's terms apply to them. The generator for
`HarborSurface.swift` is not in this repository.
